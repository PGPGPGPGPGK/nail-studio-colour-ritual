Thoughtful from start to finish

Your nail ritual.

01 / BEFORE

Come as you are.

Existing polish or extensions are fine—mention them when booking so removal time can be allowed. Bring reference photos and tell Sahithi about allergies, soreness or nail damage.

02 / DURING

Made for you.

Discuss shape, length and colour; then gentle preparation, polish or extensions, nail art, top coat and cuticle oil. Tell Sahithi immediately if anything feels painful or hot.

03 / AFTER

Care that lasts.

Use cuticle oil, wear gloves for cleaning and avoid using nails as tools. Gel polish often looks good for around two weeks; check extensions after two to three weeks.
