The nail care guide

Before & After Your Visit

Before your appointment, share reference photos and mention any existing polish, extensions, allergies, reactions, soreness or nail damage. Avoid peeling product or cutting your cuticles. During the appointment, Sahithi discusses shape, length and colour, gently prepares your nails and finishes with cuticle oil. Afterwards, use cuticle oil regularly, wear gloves for cleaning and avoid using nails as tools. If a set lifts, chips or breaks, send a photo rather than pulling it off. Gel polish can look good for about two weeks; extensions generally need a check, refill or removal after two to three weeks.
